# Review of the ternary extension (langfit/potts.py, scripts/ternary_study.py, docs/first_pass/ternary.md)

## Replace the claimed entropy certificate with an error estimate

Should-fix: “the certified entropy error (½·Newton decrement² + |g·θ|)” is not a rigorous bound, especially at the boundary that motivates the relaxed Austen tests. A read-only check with L=3, states 100/010/001 and 100 tokens each has exact maximum entropy log₂3, but potts.fit(order=2, resolution=0.001) reports converged with estimated error 3.7502905e−6 bits while its actual entropy error is 3.8755720e−6 bits; half the Newton decrement estimates a local quadratic dual gap rather than bounding it. The Austen comparisons did pass their summed estimates (order-2 difference 9.17e−7 versus 8.46e−6 bits; order-3 difference 1.27e−5 versus 4.81e−5), so the relaxation is a practical tolerance, not certification: relabel it accordingly and add an analytically solvable boundary case, or supply a genuine dual-gap bound before retaining the guarantee.

## Distinguish MAP stationarity from exact marginal matching

Should-fix: “Convergence: every marginal cell within 1% of its sampling SE” incorrectly describes the MAP fits used for held-out results and acronym landscapes. potts.py:387–388 tests μ_model−μ_emp+V/(σ²N), not μ_model−μ_emp, so shrinkage deliberately permits larger marginal discrepancies; a small A=3, L=4 MAP spot-check at σ²=0.1 converged with posterior-gradient residual 0.00260 SE but actual marginal mismatch 1.398 SE. Restrict the marginal-matching statement to exact mode and describe MAP convergence as stationarity of the penalized objective, retaining the separate max_marginal_error diagnostic.

## Limit the acronym claim to the fitted empirical distributions

Should-fix: “within this model family only three-body terms give such forms low energy without also lowering many other strings” asserts an expressivity restriction that pairwise Potts models do not have. As a direct counterexample in the existing 26-state, four-letter family, set V_ij=(10/3)(1[s_i=w_i]+1[s_j=w_j]) for w=tmrw: enumeration gives P(tmrw)=0.99547286, rank 1 and exactly one strict minimum, without any triplet term. The reported Twitter/Austen ranks remain valid, but they establish failure of these fitted pairwise distributions under these empirical constraints, not an impossibility for pair factors “however often” the acronym occurs; qualify both this conclusion and the corresponding paragraph in §4.5.

## Correct the convergence threshold and label the finite-corpus endpoint

Should-fix: “everything is converged” and “change by ≤0.003 bits per half decade from 4.5·10^6” contradict curves.Twitter: between N=4,509,851 and 14,261,400, S3 rises from 8.267242626 to 8.275225789 (0.007983 bits), S_full rises by 0.006493 bits, and I3 falls by 0.004936 bits. Moreover, the script only subsamples a fixed finite corpus and evaluates its full-data endpoint; it fits no population-size extrapolation or uncertainty for I3_∞. Move the numerical stability threshold to a range supported by the table and call 1.198829 bits the full-corpus estimate/apparent plateau, not an established infinite-data limit; distinguish subsampling stability from population bias, including the fact that Miller's observed-support correction is a bias scale rather than an error bound.

## Do not interpret overlapping-fold dispersion as calibrated significance

Should-fix: “126–590 paired SEs” treats std(fold gain)/√5 as an inferential standard error, although the five fitted predictors are dependent: each outer training set contains 80% of the same sample and two such sets share 75% of their training tokens. Pairing the losses is correctly implemented and all 35 recorded fold gains are positive, but pairing does not remove between-fold dependence; the numeric ratios therefore do not establish hundreds-of-standard-errors significance or corpus/population uncertainty. Label the displayed values as descriptive fold dispersion and remove the significance interpretation, or estimate uncertainty using independent/grouped repetitions appropriate to the sampling target; this limitation of ordinary K-fold variance estimates is documented by Bengio & Grandvalet (https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v5/grandvalet04a.html).

## State the KL Pythagorean identity without a Euclidean-length claim

Should-fix: “I^(k) are the squared lengths of mutually orthogonal components” overstates Amari's result, and the following order-independence claim can incorrectly license a per-clique decomposition. Amari explicitly distinguishes his quasi-orthogonal divergence decomposition from Euclidean components; squared Fisher distance equals twice KL only infinitesimally (Eqs. 2–3), whereas Theorem 8 concerns KL divergences (https://people.csail.mit.edu/jrennie/trg/papers/amari-ig-hierarchy-01.pdf). Keep the correct identity I^(k)=S_{k−1}−S_k=D_KL(P_k‖P_{k−1}), describe the generalized KL Pythagorean decomposition, and restrict uniqueness to the specified hierarchy of all marginals of each order; increments from adding individual cliques depend on the intermediate constraint family.

## Separate the zero-sum gauge from the lattice-gas gauge

Should-fix: “zero-sum (lattice-gas) gauge” identifies two different constructions: Ekeberg et al. Eq. 8 imposes zero sums, whereas Eq. 9 sets entries involving a reference state to zero (https://arxiv.org/html/1211.1281v2#S3.SS2). The reported dimensions are correct, but the distinction matters to the preceding claim that lower-order terms are absorbed into four triplet tensors: the implementation uses four unrestricted, overcomplete tensors, whereas four pure zero-sum triplet tensors supply only 62,500 directions; the full 66,350-dimensional family additionally contains 100 field and 3,750 pair directions. Name the gauges separately and explicitly distinguish the raw tensor parameterization from its identifiable lower-order-plus-pure-triplet decomposition.

## Describe an accurate order-three approximation, not an exact system order

Should-fix: “Four-letter English is a three-body system … and not a four-body one” is incompatible with the reported nonzero I^(4)=0.0501200 bits at full pooled N. In the cited connected-information formalism, an exactly order-three distribution has I^(4)=0; Schneidman et al. also explicitly show that marginalization/noise can create effective higher-order interactions, so fitted interactions do not identify a microscopic or linguistic generating mechanism (https://www.princeton.edu/~wbialek/our_papers/schneidman%2Bal_03b.pdf). State the supported conclusion instead: for these corpora an order-three effective model captures about 99.3% of empirical multi-information and has a substantial held-out advantage, with a small but nonzero four-body residual and finite-sample uncertainty; do not extrapolate the interaction order to English generally.

## Report small-sample near-saturation instead of a universal identity

Should-fix: “S_3 = S_full to ≤0.002 bits. Every corpus shows it” is false for the stated sample range: curves['Twitter 2010'] at N=45,099 has I4=0.00518215 bits, and several other corpora exceed 0.002; even the parenthetical universal ≤0.005 bound fails. Nor is equality “by construction” as §6 states: triplet marginal constraints generally permit a positive four-body residual, and the stored curve gaps are nonzero. State the observed near-saturation and its actual corpus-dependent range (maximum about 0.0052 bits below 45,099), restrict the 0.002 bound to pooled Twitter if desired, and remove exact-identity language from the corresponding conclusion/limitations.

## Correct the landscape multiplier for all full-corpus results

Should-fix: the summary “grows 1.7–8× with triplets”, repeated in §5, understates the reported exact-mode effect. The displayed Twitter 2023 row is 1,209/137=8.825× and Bluesky is 1,251/154=8.123×; across the study's full-year results the largest increase is Twitter 2013, 1,336/125=10.688× (detail['Twitter 2013'].full.landscape). Keep the otherwise correct exact/MAP tables and definitions, but summarize 1.7–8.8× for the displayed rows or approximately 1.7–10.7× across all analyzed corpora, explicitly stating the scope.

## Distinguish unseen words from forbidden exact-mode states

Should-fix: “unseen probes have no defined rank in exact mode” conflates absence from the sample with exclusion by an unseen clique cell. The implementation correctly ranks any finite-energy state: detail.Twitter.common.landscape.probes.rofl has count=0 but pairwise energy 30.91082164 bits and rank_2=82,115, while rank_3 is null because its triplet model forbids it. Change the limitation to say that only forbidden/infinite-energy states have null ranks; unseen but allowed words can have defined exact-mode ranks, which is central to the model's reported unseen probability mass.

## Do not equate triplet marginal ratios with whole-word probabilities

Should-fix: “Each is a frequent word … whose probability the pairwise model underestimates by a factor of 2–160” misinterprets the computed cells: μ_c/P₂|c sums over all 26 completions, rather than comparing a particular word's empirical and model probabilities. For example, the stored tim_ ratio is 1.73025, while the actual time word ratio from its count/energy is 1.86702; the _oes mass is 0.00378391, but μ(d_es)=0.00284243 already proves at least 24.9% of _oes cannot be does. Ranking these marginal residuals also does not measure sparsity of gauge-fixed three-body couplings. Label the numbers as marginal ratios, qualify the lexical conclusion as illustrative, and quantify completion concentration/word-level residuals before asserting that the interactions are predominantly sparse whole-word cores; report a consistent top-contribution selection rather than omitting mor_, t_is, _ere and _hat without explanation.

## Describe singleton-gain nonadditivity without assigning shared information

Should-fix: “about a third of what any one triplet adds is also added by the others, because each pair lies in two triplets” does not follow from the reported sum-versus-joint gains. Every singleton model already includes all six pairs, so overlapping pair constraints add no new pairwise information; furthermore, four separately fitted held-out improvements are not a decomposition assigning a common redundant fraction to each clique. Their MAP comparisons also use the disclosed order-three prior rather than separately selected singleton priors. Keep the verified ranking and sum/joint arithmetic, but call this aggregate nonadditivity under the specified fitting procedure; remove the per-clique one-third attribution and pair-count explanation, or calculate conditional/subset gains under an explicit attribution convention.
